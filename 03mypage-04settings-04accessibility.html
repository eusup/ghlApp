Accessibility Statement

We are committed to ensuring that our digital reading product is accessible to all users, including individuals with disabilities. We aim to provide an inclusive and equitable experience consistent with the principles of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and other applicable U.S. accessibility laws and regulations.

Product Overview

This product delivers educational content through a combination of:

  • Written text
  • Visual images and graphics
  • Audio narration for all core content

Accessibility Features

1. Text Accessibility
  • Clear and consistent content structure using headings and logical organization
  • Compatibility with browser and device zoom functions
  • Readable typography and sufficient color contrast to support users with low vision
2. Audio Support
  • Audio narration is available for all primary content
  • Users can listen to content instead of reading, supporting diverse learning needs
  • Playback controls (play, pause, replay) allow users to customize their experience
3. Visual Content Accessibility
  • Images are designed to support understanding of the material
  • Alternative text descriptions are provided for meaningful images to ensure compatibility with screen readers
4. Assistive Technology Compatibility
  • Designed to be compatible with common assistive technologies, including screen readers and text-to-speech tools
  • Supports keyboard navigation where applicable
5. Flexible Access
  • Content is available in multiple formats (text, audio, visuals)
  • Users can choose how they engage with the material based on their preferences and needs

Accessibility Standards

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, which are widely recognized as the standard for digital accessibility in the United States.

Ongoing Efforts

Please be aware that our efforts to improve the accessibility and usability of our website are ongoing, as technologies and requirements are continually evolving. As with all educational technology services and providers, some criteria in some accessibility standards may not be applicable to or currently supported within all of our educational products and services. We appreciate your understanding and patience.
