Privacy Policy

Highlights Library utilizes any personally identifiable information we collect for the sole purpose of individualizing the user experience within Highlights Library. In addition, from time to time, we receive from our Access Hosts information on users relating to technical issues. In such instances, we utilize the information and/or contact the user for the sole purpose of resolving the technical issue.

Information you provide to us

Certain features of Highlights Library individualize the experience, requiring the collection, use, or tracking of certain information (e.g. individual reading preferences, progress tracking, names, device identifier, IP address and/or country of residence, grade levels, reading levels and e-mail addresses). If you use Speaking activities within Highlights Library, we will ask for permission to access your computer or device's microphone. Once you complete an activity, the voice recording will be stored and made accessible to the teacher(s) and administrator(s) associated with your account for a period of 4 months. The Service does not allow users (including children) to disclose personal information publicly.

Information we collect automatically

We automatically collect certain non-personal information when you use our Service. For example, we may collect device information, such as device type, operating system type, and/or browser type. We may also collect usage data, such as what pages and content you accessed, and features you used on our Service. We may combine this device and usage information with other personal information.

Cookies and Tracking

Our mobile application(s) use a variety of technical methods for collecting and tracking device information and usage data, including cookies, pixels, and device IDs. Cookies and pixels are small pieces of text that may be saved on your device when you use our mobile application(s). We use unique identifiers in cookies to distinguish your device for purposes such as helping you navigate our mobile application(s), remembering your preferences, and understanding how our Service is used over time. Device IDs are unique identifiers for your device. We may collect and use device IDs to operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Service.

How this information is used and shared

We use the information described above solely to personalize and operate the Service, track and report learning progress to teachers and administrators, resolve technical issues, and maintain and improve the Service. We do not sell personal information collected through the Service.
